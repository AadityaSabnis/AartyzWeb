Why Aartyz

What Makes Us Different

01

Skilled Women Artisans

Our craft is led by women who have honed block printing to extraordinary mastery. Their hands, their stories, their art — in every piece you wear.

02

Bespoke Color Palettes

We celebrate our signatures, yet we are always ready to create your personal color story. No two pieces need to be alike — that is the point.

03

Modern Silhouettes

Traditional fabrics, contemporary cuts. Our designs move seamlessly from morning puja to evening dinner, from Zoom calls to summer weddings.

04

Attention to Detail

We pay close attention to the finish of every piece. We never compromise on the quality that our artisans pour their soul into.

05

Eco-Conscious Process

Natural dyes, organic fabrics, minimal waste. Sustainability is not a trend for us — it is embedded in how block printing has always been done.

06

Our Community

Our network chooses us again and again — because quality speaks. When customers reorder, it is the highest review we could ever receive.

Our Heritage

Craft rooted in
centuries of tradition

Aartyz was born in Nagpur from a profound love for India's handcrafted textile heritage. Founded by Aarti Sabnis, every collection we release is a conversation between ancient technique and modern sensibility.

"We do not just make clothing — we preserve a language that has been spoken through cloth for thousands of years."

Our artisans — predominantly skilled women — bring decades of mastery to every wooden block and every impression. Small variations from one impression to the next are part of hand printing.

How It's Made

The Aartyz Process

01

Fabric Sourcing

Premium cotton, chanderi, silk & khadi sourced from trusted mills for comfort and longevity.

02

Block Carving

Master craftsmen hand-carve intricate wooden blocks, a skill passed through generations.

03

Preparing Print Colours

We prepare the print colours in the workshop before applying them with wooden blocks. Our botanical eco prints use a separate process.

04

Hand Printing

Artisans press each block by hand, giving every piece a soul that machines cannot replicate.

05

Quality & Care

Finishing and care complete the making process.

Eco Printing

From floral waste to
wearable art

What begins as discarded flowers becomes colour, texture and story. Temple marigolds, spent roses and fallen leaves are bundled into natural cloth and steamed — the plants leave their own pigment behind, printing themselves directly onto the fabric.

The placement of each petal and leaf shapes the finished print.

Because every bundle is different, no two eco printed pieces are ever identical. Sarees, shirts, jackets and fabric by the metre — each one genuinely one of a kind.

Our Nagpur workshop

The story of craft,
color & community

Our Beginning

Born from a love
of Indian craft

Aartyz was founded by Aarti Sabnis in Nagpur with one powerful belief: India's textile heritage deserves a modern platform. What started as a small workshop fueled by passion grew into a workshop that prints, finishes and makes textiles in Nagpur.

We work hand-in-hand with women artisans who have dedicated their lives to block printing. Every piece carries their story — their precision, their creativity, their legacy.

Partner With Us

What We Stand For

Our Values

🌱

Sustainability

Eco-conscious dyes, natural fabrics, and waste-minimal processes are not add-ons — they are the foundation of how block printing has always worked.

👐

Empowerment

Our artisans — predominantly women — are paid fairly, work in dignified conditions, and grow their craft alongside our brand every season.

🏺

Preservation

Block print is a language. We are committed to keeping it alive, relevant, and celebrated — passing it forward in every collection we release.

The Founder

Aarti Sabnis

Founder & Creative Director

"Every block print is a conversation between the artisan and the wearer — a thread of culture that connects us across time. Aartyz exists to make that connection louder."

Aarti has built Aartyz from the ground up, combining her deep reverence for Indian craft with a sharp eye for contemporary design. She leads every collection with the belief that the most beautiful fashion is also the most honest.

Delivery, returns & privacy

Delivery within India

Enter your delivery PIN code at checkout to see available methods and charges. Check the product details for any listed dispatch time. Confirm timing with us for custom and bulk orders.

Returns and order issues

The store’s 10-day window applies to eligible items. Sarees, accessories, home décor, fabrics, discounted purchases and some made-to-order items have exclusions. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact ARTYZ promptly; the published policy asks customers to report damage within 24 hours.
